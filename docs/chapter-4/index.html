Chapter 4: the enriched graphical calculus

Dara O Shayda and Noor Hoshino (星野ヌール)

After Turaev & Virelizier, “Monoidal Categories and Topological Field Theory”, Chapter 4 (§4.3.2 and §4.5.2–4.7, pp. 70–87).

Each page below has one result: the statement, then the proof as a chain of steps. Each step is an example in the editor, and Simplify uses the rule the book cites for that step. Steps about single basis vectors, and Lemmas 4.6, 4.7 and 4.9(e)–(g), are checked in matrices in the Wolfram notebook graphical-calculus-4.nb. Its model is a category of graded vector spaces, pivotal but not spherical, so the left and right dimensions differ.
