What it does

Box-and-arcs editor

Boxes, boxes with source or target 𝟙, dots, arc ends and “=” signs. Drag between ports to draw arcs, edit colours, undo, save and export SVG.

Maths keypad

⊗, ∘, 𝟙, ∨X, id, ev, coev, ω, Ω, Ω′, Greek letters and subscripts, typeset as in the book while you type.

Expression ⇄ diagram

Turns (idY ⊗ g)(f ⊗ idU) into a picture and reads a picture back as an expression, side by side for equations.

Simplifier, two engines

Level exchange (Fig. 2.1), the (2.1) zigzags, the definition of ∨f and the (2.2) slides, step by step. It runs offline in JavaScript, or on the Wolfram notebook engine in the cloud, and the two engines are checked against each other.

Conventions (from §2.1)

  • Arcs are oriented downward: sources at the top, targets at the bottom. A toggle flips the direction.
  • Composition is written by juxtaposition, gf, and binds tighter than ⊗. Separate the sides of an equation with =.
  • The unit is 𝟙, and its strands are erased. Every morphism is drawn as a box.
  • Built in: evX : ∨X ⊗ X → 𝟙 and coevX : 𝟙 → X ⊗ ∨X. Declare a pairing with its inverse with pairing ω Ω.

Wolfram engine

The “Wolfram kernel” option sends the diagram's layered form to GraphicalCalculusKernel, a Wolfram Cloud API that runs the rules of the notebook graphical-calculus-2.1-simplify.nb. To redeploy it, evaluate kernel/GraphicalCalculusKernel-deploy.wl in a signed-in Wolfram session.
